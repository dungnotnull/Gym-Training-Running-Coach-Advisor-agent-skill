# Warming Up & Mobility for Injury Prevention

## Research Foundation

**Primary Sources:**
1. ACSM (2021). ACSM's Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription
2. National Academy of Sports Medicine (NASM) (2021). Essentials of Personal Fitness Training
3. Review literature on dynamic vs. static stretching for performance

## Core Principles

**Purpose of Warm-Up:**
1. Increase body temperature
2. Increase blood flow to working muscles
3. Prepare nervous system for movement
4. Reduce injury risk
5. Improve performance

**Two-Part Structure:**
1. **General Warm-Up:** Cardiovascular warm-up (2-5 minutes)
2. **Specific Warm-Up:** Dynamic stretching and movement prep (3-8 minutes)

---

## General Warm-Up (Cardiovascular)

### Purpose

Raise core body temperature and prepare cardiovascular system for increased demand.

### Guidelines

- Duration: 2-5 minutes
- Intensity: Light (RPE 11-12, 40-50% max HR)
- Activity: Any rhythmic, full-body movement

### Activities

**Treadmill/Outdoor:**
- Brisk walking
- Slow jogging
- Walking with arm swings

**Cardio Equipment:**
- Cycling (light resistance)
- Elliptical
- Rowing machine
- Stair climbing (slow pace)

**Bodyweight:**
- Jumping jacks (low impact)
- High knees (march, not run)
- Butt kicks (slow)
- Arm circles while stepping

### Examples

**Before Strength Training:**
- 3 minutes brisk walking
- 2 minutes light cycling

**Before Running:**
- 3-5 minutes slow walking → jogging progression

**Before Intervals:**
- 5 minutes easy jogging with gradual pace increase

## Specific Warm-Up (Dynamic Stretching)

### Purpose

Prepare specific muscles and movement patterns for the upcoming activity.

### Guidelines

- Duration: 5-10 minutes
- Type: Dynamic (movement-based) stretching
- Intensity: Gradually increasing range of motion
- Focus: Movement patterns similar to training session

### Dynamic Stretching Exercises

#### Lower Body Dynamic Stretches

**1. Leg Swings**
- Purpose: Hip mobility, hamstring flexibility
- How: Hold onto support, swing leg front-back and side-to-side
- Reps: 10-15 swings per direction per leg
- Cues: Keep torso upright, smooth movement

**2. Walking Lunges**
- Purpose: Hip mobility, glute activation, leg strength
- How: Step forward into lunge, push through front heel to next lunge
- Reps: 10-15 lunges per leg
- Cues: Keep chest up, knees tracking over toes

**3. Bodyweight Squats**
- Purpose: Hip mobility, glute activation, warm-up for squat pattern
- How: Feet shoulder-width, squat to comfortable depth, stand
- Reps: 15-20 reps
- Cues: Weight in heels, chest up, knees push out

**4. Lateral Lunges**
- Purpose: Adductor mobility, lateral hip stability
- How: Step wide to side, shift weight to that leg, other leg straight
- Reps: 8-10 reps per side
- Cues: Keep heels down, chest up, smooth movement

**5. Hip Circles**
- Purpose: Hip joint mobility
- How: Standing or hands-and-knees, make circles with one leg
- Reps: 10 circles each direction per leg
- Cues: Smooth controlled movement, full range of motion

**6. Inchworms (Walkouts)**
- Purpose: Shoulder stability, core activation, hamstring stretch
- How: Stand tall, walk hands out to plank position, walk feet to hands
- Reps: 5-8 reps
- Cues: Keep core tight, control the movement

#### Upper Body Dynamic Stretches

**1. Arm Circles**
- Purpose: Shoulder mobility
- How: Arms straight, make circles (small to large)
- Reps: 10 circles forward, 10 backward
- Cues: Keep shoulders relaxed, smooth movement

**2. Arm Swings (Horizontal and Vertical)**
- Purpose: Shoulder and chest mobility
- How: Swing arms forward-back and across body
- Reps: 15 swings per direction
- Cues: Use momentum, don't force range

**3. Shoulder Rolls**
- Purpose: Upper trap and shoulder mobility
- How: Roll shoulders forward and backward in circles
- Reps: 10 rolls each direction
- Cues: Smooth, controlled movement

**4. Band Pull-Aparts (if using band)**
- Purpose: Scapular stability, rotator cuff activation
- How: Hold light band, pull apart squeezing shoulder blades
- Reps: 15-20 reps
- Cues: Control the return, squeeze shoulder blades

**5. Push-up Plus**
- Purpose: Core stability, scapular protraction, chest activation
- How: Push-up position, extra push at top (protract shoulder blades)
- Reps: 10-15 reps
- Cues: Keep core tight, control the movement

#### Core Dynamic Stretches

**1. Cat-Cow**
- Purpose: Spinal mobility, core activation
- How: Hands and knees, arch back (cat), then round back (cow)
- Reps: 10-15 reps
- Cues: Move with breath, smooth transitions

**2. Bird Dog**
- Purpose: Core stability, coordination
- How: Hands and knees, extend opposite arm and leg
- Reps: 8-10 reps per side
- Cues: Keep back flat, don't hyperextend

**3. Dead Bug**
- Purpose: Core stability, coordination
- How: Lying face up, opposite arm and leg lower and raise
- Reps: 10-15 reps per side
- Cues: Keep low back pressed to floor, slow movement

**4. Plank Walkouts**
- Purpose: Core stability, shoulder stability
- How: Plank position, walk feet in and out
- Reps: 8-10 reps
- Cues: Keep hips level, control movement

## Exercise-Specific Warm-Ups

### Before Squat Session

1. **General:** 3-5 minutes light cardio
2. **Bodyweight Squats:** 2x15 reps
3. **Lateral Lunges:** 10 reps per side
4. **Hip Circles:** 10 each direction per leg
5. **Leg Swings:** 15 swings per leg
6. **Working Up to Weight:** 2-3 progressively heavier warm-up sets

### Before Bench Press Session

1. **General:** 3-5 minutes light cardio
2. **Arm Circles:** 10 each direction
3. **Band Pull-Aparts:** 2x15
4. **Push-up Plus:** 2x10
5. **Light Bench:** 2x10 with empty bar
6. **Working Up to Weight:** 2-3 progressively heavier warm-up sets

### Before Deadlift Session

1. **General:** 3-5 minutes light cardio
2. **Bodyweight Squats:** 2x15
3. **Hip Circles:** 10 each direction per leg
4. **Leg Swings:** 15 swings per leg
5. **Cat-Cow:** 15 reps
6. **Light Deadlift:** 2x5 with empty bar or light weight

### Before Running Session

1. **General:** 5 minutes easy jogging
2. **Walking Lunges:** 15 per leg
3. **Leg Swings:** 20 swings per leg
4. **High Knees (Marching):** 20 yards
5. **Butt Kicks:** 20 yards
6. **A-Skips and B-Skips:** 20 yards each
7. **Strides:** 3x20-30 second accelerations

### Before Interval Session

1. **General:** 5-8 minutes easy jogging
2. **Dynamic Stretches:** 5 minutes (leg swings, lunges, hip circles)
3. **Strides:** 3-4x30 seconds at gradually increasing intensity
4. **Rest:** 1-2 minutes before first interval

## Static Stretching (Post-Exercise or Separate Sessions)

### When to Use Static Stretching

**Appropriate Times:**
- Post-exercise cool-down
- Separate dedicated flexibility sessions
- Before bed for relaxation

**Inappropriate Times:**
- Pre-exercise warm-up (can reduce power output)
- Immediately before strength/power training

### Major Muscle Static Stretches

#### Lower Body Stretches

**1. Hamstring Stretch**
- Purpose: Hamstring flexibility, low back health
- How: Seated or standing, reach toward toes
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps
- Cues: Keep back flat, hinge at hips

**2. Quad Stretch**
- Purpose: Quadriceps flexibility
- How: Standing, pull ankle toward glutes
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps per leg
- Cues: Keep knees together, don't arch back

**3. Hip Flexor Stretch**
- Purpose: Hip flexor flexibility, hip health
- How: Half-kneeling, push hips forward
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps per leg
- Cues: Squeeze glute on kneeling side

**4. Calf Stretch**
- Purpose: Calf flexibility, Achilles health
- How: Hands on wall, one foot back, press heel down
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps per leg
- Cues: Keep back leg straight, press heel into ground

**5. Butterfly Stretch**
- Purpose: Adductor flexibility
- How: Seated, soles of feet together, knees pushed down
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps
- Cues: Keep back straight, gentle pressure

**6. Figure-4 Stretch**
- Purpose: Glute flexibility
- How: Supine, one ankle crossed over opposite knee, pull legs to chest
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps per side
- Cues: Keep head on ground, don't force

#### Upper Body Stretches

**1. Chest Stretch**
- Purpose: Chest and anterior shoulder flexibility
- How: In doorway, arms up at 90 degrees, lean forward
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps
- Cues: Keep shoulders down, gentle stretch

**2. Lat Stretch**
- Purpose: Latissimus dorsi flexibility
- How: Hands on wall at waist height, lean back, hinge at hips
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps
- Cues: Keep chest toward ground, flat back

**3. Shoulder Stretch**
- Purpose: Posterior shoulder flexibility
- How: Arm across body, pull toward chest
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps per arm
- Cues: Keep arm straight, gentle pressure

**4. Tricep Stretch**
- Purpose: Tricep and shoulder flexibility
- How: Arm overhead, elbow bent, pull elbow toward head
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps per arm
- Cues: Keep elbow pointing forward, gentle pressure

**5. Wrist Flexor/Extensor Stretches**
- Purpose: Forearm flexibility, injury prevention
- How: Arm extended, pull hand back (extend) or forward (flex)
- Hold: 15-30 seconds, 2-4 reps per position per arm
- Cues: Keep elbow straight, gentle stretch

## Foam Rolling (Self-Myofascial Release)

### Purpose

Release muscle tension, improve tissue mobility, aid recovery.

### Guidelines

- Duration: 30-60 seconds per muscle group
- Frequency: Can be used daily, especially post-exercise
- Intensity: Moderate discomfort, not severe pain
- Avoid: Rolling directly over joints or bony prominences

### Major Areas to Foam Roll

**1. Calves**
- How: Seated, calf on roller, roll from ankle to knee
- Duration: 30-60 seconds per leg

**2. Hamstrings**
- How: Seised, hamstring on roller, roll from glute to knee
- Duration: 30-60 seconds per leg

**3. Quads**
- How: Plank position, quad on roller, roll from hip to knee
- Duration: 30-60 seconds per leg

**4. IT Band**
- How: Side-lying, IT band on roller, roll from hip to knee
- Duration: 30-60 seconds per side
- Note: Often uncomfortable, start with less pressure

**5. Glutes**
- How: Seated on roller, one ankle crossed over opposite knee, roll glute
- Duration: 30-60 seconds per side

**6. Upper Back (Thoracic Spine)**
- How: Lying on back, roller at shoulder blade level, roll up/down
- Duration: 30-60 seconds
- Note: Avoid lower back (lumbar spine)

**7. Lats**
- How: Lying on side, lat on roller, roll from armpit to lower rib
- Duration: 30-60 seconds per side

## Mobility Work

### What is Mobility?

Mobility = Flexibility + Strength through range of motion. It's not just being able to reach a position, but having control there.

### Mobility Exercises

**1. Hip Openers (90/90)**
- How: Seised, knees at 90 degrees, lift one knee and cross over
- Reps: 8-10 reps per side
- Purpose: Hip rotation, glute activation

**2. Thoracic Spine Rotations**
- How: Hands and knees, one hand behind head, rotate elbow to ceiling
- Reps: 10 reps per side
- Purpose: Thoracic spine mobility

**3. Ankle Mobility**
- How: Standing, foot in band or against wall, drive knee forward
- Reps: 10-15 reps per ankle
- Purpose: Ankle dorsiflexion, squat depth improvement

**4. Shoulder CARs (Controlled Articular Rotations)**
- How: Standing or lying, make slow controlled circles with arm
- Reps: 5 circles each direction per arm
- Purpose: Shoulder joint health, mobility

**5. Wrist CARs**
- How: Hand flat on surface, make circles with forearm
- Reps: 5 circles each direction per wrist
- Purpose: Wrist health, injury prevention

## Sample Warm-Up Protocols

### Before Strength Training (10 minutes)

**0-3 min:** Brisk walking or light cycling
**3-6 min:** Dynamic stretches (arm circles, bodyweight squats, leg swings)
**6-8 min:** Exercise-specific movements (band pull-aparts for upper, bodyweight squats for lower)
**8-10 min:** Light warm-up sets with empty bar or light weights

### Before Running (12 minutes)

**0-5 min:** Easy walking to jogging progression
**5-8 min:** Dynamic stretches (leg swings, walking lunges, hip circles)
**8-10 min:** Running-specific drills (high knees, butt kicks, A-skips)
**10-12 min:** Strides (2-3x20 seconds at gradually increasing pace)

### Before Interval Session (15 minutes)

**0-5 min:** Easy jogging
**5-10 min:** Dynamic stretches and drills
**10-12 min:** Strides at increasing intensity
**12-15 min:** Rest before first interval

## Common Warm-Up Mistakes

1. **Skipping warm-up entirely:** Going straight into intense exercise
2. **Static stretching before exercise:** Can reduce power output
3. **Too long warm-up:** Spending 20+ minutes, depleting energy
4. **Too short warm-up:** Less than 5 minutes, insufficient preparation
5. **Wrong intensity:** Warm-up too hard or too easy
6. **Ignoring injury-prone areas:** Not focusing on previously injured areas
7. **Inconsistency:** Not warming up consistently before every session

## Injury Prevention Guidelines

### General Principles

1. **Progressive Overload:** Gradually increase training demands
2. **Adequate Recovery:** Allow 48 hours between same muscle group training
3. **Proper Technique:** Prioritize form over load or speed
4. **Appropriate Warm-Up:** Always include proper warm-up
5. **Listen to Body:** Respect pain and warning signs
6. **Balance Training:** Balance push/pull, upper/lower, agonist/antagonist
7. **Appropriate Footwear:** Wear proper shoes for activity
8. **Environmental Awareness:** Adjust training for heat, cold, humidity

### Red Flags Warning Signs

**Immediate Action Required:**
- Sharp pain (vs. dull ache)
- Pain that changes movement pattern
- Pain that increases with activity
- Swelling or visible deformity
- Loss of function or range of motion
- Numbness or tingling

**Medical Evaluation Needed For:**
- Pain persisting >2 weeks despite rest
- Recurring pain in same location
- Pain interfering with daily activities
- Pain accompanied by other symptoms (fever, etc.)

## Warm-Up & Mobility Checklist

Before finalizing warm-up protocol:

- [ ] General cardiovascular warm-up included (2-5 min)
- [ ] Dynamic stretching appropriate to activity
- [ ] Exercise-specific movement prep included
- [ ] Total warm-up duration 5-15 minutes
- [ ] Static stretching reserved for post-exercise or separate sessions
- [ ] Mobility work integrated for stiff joints
- [ ] Warm-up intensity appropriate (light to moderate)
- [ ] Individualized for previous injuries or mobility limitations
- [ ] Consistent across all training sessions
- [ ] Sufficiently prepares for main session demands
