# ACSM Exercise Prescription Guidelines

## Research Foundation

**Primary Sources:**
1. American College of Sports Medicine (2021). ACSM's Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription (11th ed.)
2. ACSM (2009). Progression Models in Resistance Training for Healthy Adults
3. W. Kraemer, N. Ratamess (2004). Fundamentals of Resistance Training

## Core Framework: FITT-VP

The ACSM prescribes exercise using the FITT-VP framework:

- **F**requency: How often
- **I**ntensity: How hard
- **T**ime: How long
- **T**ype: What modality
- **V**olume: Total work
- **P**rogression: How to advance

## FITT-VP for Resistance Training

### Frequency

**Beginner (0-6 months):**
- 2-3 days per week
- At least 48 hours between same muscle group sessions
- Full body or split routine

**Intermediate (6-12 months):**
- 3-4 days per week
- 48 hours between same muscle group sessions
- Upper/lower or body part splits possible

**Advanced (12+ months):**
- 4-6 days per week
- Can train same muscle group more frequently with proper periodization
- Specialized splits (push/pull, body part, etc.)

### Intensity

**Measuring Intensity Methods:**

**1. Percentage of 1-Repetition Maximum (%1RM):**
- Beginner: 60-70% 1RM (focus on learning form)
- Intermediate: 70-85% 1RM
- Advanced: 80-95% 1RM for strength

**2. Repetition Maximum (RM) Zones:**
- Strength: 1-5 RM (85-100% 1RM)
- Power: 1-5 RM (30-45% 1RM for speed, 80-90% for force)
- Hypertrophy: 6-12 RM (67-85% 1RM)
- Endurance: 15+ RM (≤67% 1RM)

**3. Rating of Perceived Exertion (RPE) - Borg Scale 6-20:**
- 6: Very, very light
- 11: Light
- 13: Somewhat hard
- 15: Hard
- 17: Very hard
- 20: Maximal exertion

**4. OMNI Resistance Exercise Scale (0-10):**
- 0-1: Very easy
- 2-3: Easy
- 4-6: Somewhat hard to hard
- 7-8: Very hard
- 9-10: Extremely hard/maximal

### Time

**Duration per Session:**
- Beginner: 20-30 minutes
- Intermediate: 30-60 minutes
- Advanced: 60-90 minutes

**Note:** Training duration includes warm-up, main session, cool-down

### Type

**Exercise Categories:**

**1. Primary Exercises (Multi-joint, Compound):**
- Squat variations
- Deadlift variations
- Bench press variations
- Row variations
- Overhead press variations
- Pull-up/lat pulldown

**2. Secondary Exercises (Single-joint, Assistance):**
- Leg extension/curl
- Chest flys
- Lateral raises
- Bicep curls
- Tricep extensions
- Core exercises

**Exercise Selection Guidelines:**
- Prioritize primary exercises for each session
- Include 1-2 primary exercises per muscle group
- Add 2-4 secondary exercises per session as needed

### Volume

**Sets per Exercise:**
- Beginner: 1-3 sets per exercise
- Intermediate: 2-4 sets per exercise
- Advanced: 3-6 sets per exercise

**Reps per Set:**
- Strength: 1-5 reps
- Hypertrophy: 6-12 reps
- Endurance: 15+ reps
- Power: 1-5 reps (light for speed, moderate for force)

**Total Volume per Session:**
- Beginner: 12-20 sets total
- Intermediate: 20-30 sets total
- Advanced: 30-40 sets total

### Progression

**Progression Stages:**

**Stage 1: Stabilization (Beginner, weeks 1-4)**
- Focus: Movement pattern learning
- Volume: Low
- Intensity: Low
- Frequency: 2-3x/week

**Stage 2: Muscular Endurance (weeks 5-12)**
- Focus: Building work capacity
- Volume: Moderate
- Intensity: Low-moderate (50-70% 1RM)
- Frequency: 2-3x/week

**Stage 3: Hypertrophy (weeks 13-24)**
- Focus: Muscle growth
- Volume: High
- Intensity: Moderate-high (70-85% 1RM)
- Frequency: 3-4x/week

**Stage 4: Maximal Strength (weeks 25+)**
- Focus: Maximal force production
- Volume: Low-moderate
- Intensity: High (85-100% 1RM)
- Frequency: 3-6x/week

**Progression Criteria:**
- Increase load when target reps can be completed with good form
- Typical increments: 2.5-10 lbs depending on exercise
- Deload every 4-8 weeks

## FITT-VP for Cardiovascular Training

### Frequency

**Beginner:**
- 3-5 days per week
- Start at lower end, build gradually

**Intermediate/Advanced:**
- 3-7 days per week
- Depends on intensity and goals

### Intensity

**Measuring Intensity Methods:**

**1. Heart Rate Reserve (Karvonen Formula):**
```
Target HR = ((HRmax - HRrest) × %Intensity) + HRrest
HRmax ≈ 220 - age (or use field test)
HRrest = Measured resting heart rate
```

**Intensity Zones:**
- Very Light: 20-39% HRR
- Light: 40-59% HRR
- Moderate: 60-69% HRR
- Vigorous: 70-89% HRR
- Near Maximal: ≥90% HRR

**2. Talk Test:**
- Moderate: Can converse in short sentences
- Vigorous: Can say 2-3 words without gasping

**3. RPE (Borg Scale 6-20):**
- Moderate: 12-13
- Vigorous: 14-17

### Time

**Beginner:**
- Start: 10-15 minutes per session
- Build to: 30 minutes per session
- Progression: Add 5 minutes every 1-2 weeks

**Intermediate/Advanced:**
- Moderate intensity: 30-60 minutes
- Vigorous intensity: 20-40 minutes
- Interval training: Varies by protocol

**Weekly Minimums:**
- Health benefits: 150-300 minutes moderate OR 75-150 minutes vigorous
- Weight management: 250-300 minutes moderate OR 125-150 minutes vigorous
- Weight loss: >250 minutes per week

### Type

**Activity Categories:**

**Primary Activities:**
- Walking, jogging, running
- Cycling
- Swimming
- Rowing
- Elliptical
- Stair climbing

**Secondary Activities:**
- Group fitness classes
- Sports (basketball, soccer, etc.)
- Hiking
- Dance

**Selection Guidelines:**
- Choose activities athlete enjoys
- Consider accessibility and equipment
- Mix primary and secondary for variety

### Volume

**Weekly Targets:**
- Minimum for health: 150 min moderate OR 75 min vigorous
- Improved fitness: 200-300 min moderate OR 100-150 min vigorous
- Weight loss: >250 min moderate OR >150 min vigorous

### Progression

**Cardiovascular Progression:**
- Duration first (build to 30-60 min sessions)
- Then intensity (increase to moderate or vigorous)
- Then volume (add sessions per week)

**Rate of Progression:**
- Duration: Add 5-10 min per week
- Intensity: Small increases every 1-2 weeks
- Frequency: Add 1 session per week every 2-3 weeks

## FITT-VP for Flexibility Training

### Frequency
- 2-3 days per week minimum
- Daily is acceptable and beneficial

### Intensity
- Stretch to point of mild discomfort, not pain
- Hold position without bouncing

### Time
- Static stretches: Hold 10-30 seconds
- Repeat 2-4 times per stretch
- Total session: 10-20 minutes

### Type
**Stretching Categories:**
- Static stretching
- Dynamic stretching
- PNF (proprioceptive neuromuscular facilitation)
- Ballistic (NOT recommended for most populations)

**Selection:**
- Dynamic: Pre-workout warm-up
- Static/PNF: Post-workout or separate sessions

### Volume
- Stretch major muscle groups
- 2-4 sets per stretch
- 10-30 seconds per set

### Progression
- Increase hold duration gradually (10 → 30 sec)
- Increase range of motion as flexibility improves
- Add advanced stretches (PNF) as proficiency develops

## Exercise Programming Templates

### Beginner Full Body Routine (3x/week)

**Frequency:** 3 days per week (Mon/Wed/Fri)

**Day 1, 3, 5 (Full Body):**
1. Goblet Squat: 2x12 (learning form)
2. Push-up (modified if needed): 2x10
3. Dumbbell Row: 2x12
4. Overhead Press (light dumbbells): 2x10
5. Glute Bridge: 2x15
6. Plank: 2x20 seconds

**Progression:**
- Week 1-2: Focus on form, light weights
- Week 3-4: Add 1 set per exercise
- Week 5-6: Begin load progression
- Week 7-8: Deload
- Week 9+: Progressive overload

### Intermediate Upper/Lower Split (4x/week)

**Frequency:** 4 days per week (Mon/Tue/Thu/Fri)

**Monday (Upper A):**
- Bench Press: 3x8
- Bent Over Row: 3x10
- Overhead Press: 3x8
- Pull-ups/Lat Pulldown: 3x10
- Bicep Curls: 2x12
- Tricep Extensions: 2x12

**Tuesday (Lower A):**
- Squat: 3x8
- Romanian Deadlift: 3x10
- Leg Press: 3x12
- Leg Curls: 3x12
- Calf Raises: 3x15
- Plank: 3x30 seconds

**Thursday (Upper B):**
- Incline Bench: 3x10
- Seated Cable Row: 3x10
- Lateral Raises: 3x12
- Face Pulls: 3x15
- Hammer Curls: 2x12
- Skullcrushers: 2x12

**Friday (Lower B):**
- Deadlift: 3x5
- Front Squat: 3x8
- Lunges: 3x10 each
- Leg Extensions: 3x12
- Calf Raises: 3x15
- Hanging Leg Raises: 3x10

### Advanced Push/Pull/Legs Split (6x/week)

**Frequency:** 6 days per week (Mon-Sat)

**Monday (Push):**
- Bench Press: 4x6
- Overhead Press: 4x8
- Incline Dumbbell Press: 3x10

- Tricep Pushdown: 3x12

**Tuesday (Pull):**
- Deadlift: 4x5
- Pull-ups: 4x8
- Barbell Row: 4x8
- Face Pulls: 3x15
- Bicep Curls: 3x12
- Hammer Curls: 3x12

**Wednesday (Legs):**
- Squat: 4x6
- Leg Press: 4x10
- Romanian Deadlift: 3x10
- Leg Curls: 3x12

- Calf Raises: 4x15

**Thursday (Push):**
- Incline Bench: 4x8
- Seated Dumbbell Press: 3x10

## Special Populations Considerations

### Older Adults (65+ years)

**Modifications:**
- Focus on balance and fall prevention
- Include balance exercises 2-3x/week
- Resistance training 2-3x/week
- May need longer recovery (48-72 hours between sessions)
- Focus on functional movement patterns

### Youth (<18 years)

**Modifications:**
- Focus on proper form and movement patterns
- Avoid maximal single-rep testing
- Supervised training recommended
- Focus on bodyweight exercises initially
- Progressive resistance introduction

### Pregnancy (Post-Partum)

**Modifications:**
- Consult healthcare provider before exercising
- Avoid supine positions after first trimester
- Avoid activities with fall risk
- Monitor intensity (able to talk comfortably)
- Focus on maintenance, not progression

### Chronic Disease

**Modifications:**
- Medical clearance recommended
- May need lower starting intensities
- Slower progression
- More frequent monitoring of response
- Consider working with clinical exercise specialist

## Warm-Up and Cool-Down Guidelines

### Warm-Up (5-10 minutes)

**Purpose:** Prepare body for exercise, reduce injury risk

**Components:**
1. **Cardiovascular (2-5 minutes):**
   - Light activity (walking, cycling)
   - RPE 11-12 (light)

2. **Dynamic Stretching (3-5 minutes):**
   - Movement-based stretches
   - Sport-specific movements
   - Active range of motion

**Example Warm-Up:**
- 3 minutes light jogging
- Arm circles (30 sec each direction)
- Leg swings (30 sec each leg)
- Bodyweight squats (15 reps)
- Walking lunges (10 each leg)

### Cool-Down (5-10 minutes)

**Purpose:** Gradual return to resting state

**Components:**
1. **Light Cardiovascular (2-3 minutes):**
   - Very light activity (walking)
   - Gradually reduce intensity

2. **Static Stretching (3-5 minutes):**
   - Hold each stretch 15-30 seconds
   - 2-4 reps per stretch
   - Major muscle groups worked

**Example Cool-Down:**
- 3 minutes light walking
- Hamstring stretch (30 sec each leg)
- Quad stretch (30 sec each leg)
- Chest stretch (30 sec each arm)
- Shoulder stretch (30 sec each arm)
- Calf stretch (30 sec each leg)

## ACSM Programming Checklist

Before finalizing an exercise program:

- [ ] FITT-VP principles applied appropriately
- [ ] Intensity appropriate for experience level
- [ ] Frequency allows for adequate recovery
- [ ] Exercise selection balanced (push/pull, upper/lower)
- [ ] Warm-up included in each session
- [ ] Cool-down included in each session
- [ ] Progression criteria clearly defined
- [ ] Deload/recovery periods scheduled
- [ ] Special population considerations addressed
- [ ] Safety precautions included

---

## Common Programming Mistakes

1. **Progressing too fast:** >10% increase per week in volume
2. **Skipping warm-up:** Jumping into intense exercise
3. **Inadequate recovery:** <48 hours between same muscle group training
4. **Imbalanced training:** Overemphasizing certain muscle groups
5. **Poor exercise selection:** Choosing advanced exercises for beginners
6. **Ignoring intensity:** Not pushing hard enough or too hard
7. **No progression:** Stagnant program for too long
8. **Overreaching:** Training too hard without adequate recovery
