# Strength Training Program Templates

## Overview

This document contains template structures for strength training programs using different periodization models. These templates are grounded in exercise physiology research and sports science principles.

---

## Linear Periodization (Beginner)

### Model Description

Linear periodization is ideal for beginners as it provides structured progression with clear phases. It follows the principle of progressive overload by gradually increasing intensity while decreasing volume over time.

### Phase Structure

#### Phase 1: Hypertrophy/Endurance (Weeks 1-4)

**Goal**: Build work capacity and muscle mass

**Parameters**:
- **Intensity**: 65% 1RM
- **Volume**: High (3 sets × 10-12 reps)
- **Rest Interval**: 90 seconds between sets
- **Focus**: Muscular endurance and work capacity
- **Deload**: Week 4 (50% volume reduction)

**Key Exercises**:
- Back Squat: 3 × 10-12, 90s rest
- Bench Press: 3 × 10-12, 90s rest
- Deadlift: 3 × 10-12, 90s rest
- Overhead Press: 3 × 10-12, 90s rest
- Barbell Row: 3 × 10-12, 90s rest

#### Phase 2: Strength (Weeks 5-8)

**Goal**: Build maximal strength

**Parameters**:
- **Intensity**: 80% 1RM
- **Volume**: Moderate (4 sets × 5-8 reps)
- **Rest Interval**: 3 minutes between sets
- **Focus**: Neural adaptation and strength development
- **Deload**: Week 8 (50% volume reduction)

**Key Exercises**:
- Back Squat: 4 × 5-8, 3min rest
- Bench Press: 3 × 5-8, 3min rest
- Deadlift: 3 × 5-8, 3min rest
- Overhead Press: 3 × 5-8, 3min rest
- Barbell Row: 3 × 5-8, 3min rest

#### Phase 3: Peaking (Weeks 9-12)

**Goal**: Express maximal strength

**Parameters**:
- **Intensity**: 90% 1RM
- **Volume**: Low (3 sets × 2-5 reps)
- **Rest Interval**: 5 minutes between sets
- **Focus**: Maximal force production
- **Deload**: Week 12 (recommended)

**Key Exercises**:
- Back Squat: 3 × 3-5, 5min rest
- Bench Press: 3 × 3-5, 5min rest
- Deadlift: 3 × 3-5, 5min rest
- Overhead Press: 3 × 3-5, 5min rest
- Barbell Row: 3 × 3-5, 5min rest

### Progression Rules

1. **Load Increases**: Add weight when able to complete the top end of the rep range with proper form
2. **Deload Protocol**: Every 4th week, reduce training volume by 50% while maintaining intensity
3. **Intensity Progression**: Follow linear increase: 65% → 80% → 90% 1RM

### Training Schedule

**Default**: 3 days per week (Monday, Wednesday, Friday)

**Alternative**: 4 days per week (Monday, Tuesday, Thursday, Friday)

Each session includes:
- **Warm-up**: 5-10 minutes light cardio + dynamic stretching
- **Main Work**: Compound movements as listed above
- **Cool-down**: 5-10 minutes static stretching

### Research Foundation

This linear periodization model is supported by:

1. **Progressive Overload Principle**: Gradually increasing stress on the body leads to adaptations (Kraemer & Ratamess, 2004)

2. **Periodization Research**: Structured training with planned variation produces superior results compared to non-periodized training (Harvey et al., 2011)

3. **Deload Benefits**: Strategic reduction in training volume prevents overtraining and promotes supercompensation (Stone et al., 2009)

4. **Beginner Adaptations**: Novices experience rapid neurological and structural adaptations with linear progression (Moritani & deVries, 1979)

## Important Disclaimer

These templates provide general educational information about strength training programming. They are not substitutes for:

1. **Professional Coaching**: In-person coaching is essential for proper technique and form correction
2. **Medical Clearance**: Consult a physician before starting any new exercise program
3. **Individual Assessment**: Programs should be adjusted based on individual response, injury history, and specific goals

Always prioritize proper form over load increases, and discontinue any exercise that causes pain (as opposed to discomfort).
