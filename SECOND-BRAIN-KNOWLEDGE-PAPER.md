# SECOND-BRAIN-KNOWLEDGE-PAPER.md — Gym Training & Running Coach Advisor

A curated knowledge base of research papers, books, and authoritative sources underpinning this skill's methodology. These should be consulted (and their principles extracted into skill reference files) when building the actual skill.

> **Note on sourcing:** This list was compiled from general subject-matter knowledge to give the skill a strong research foundation to build from. Before relying on any specific citation (e.g., in an academic, legal, or professional deliverable), verify the exact title, year, and venue independently, as this list has not been individually re-verified against live databases at compilation time.

## Research Papers & Sources (20)

1. T. Delorme (1945). Restoration of Muscle Power by Heavy-Resistance Exercises. Journal of Bone and Joint Surgery — Foundational progressive-overload strength-training methodology.
2. American College of Sports Medicine (2021). ACSM's Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription (11th ed.). Wolters Kluwer — Standard evidence-based exercise-prescription reference.
3. S. Seiler (2010). What Is Best Practice for Training Intensity and Duration Distribution in Endurance Athletes? International Journal of Sports Physiology and Performance — Foundational polarized-training-model research.
4. G. Zatsiorsky, W. Kraemer (2006). Science and Practice of Strength Training (2nd ed.). Human Kinetics — Comprehensive strength-training periodization and program-design reference.
5. T. Bompa, C. Buzzichelli (2018). Periodization: Theory and Methodology of Training (6th ed.). Human Kinetics — Standard comprehensive periodization-theory textbook.
6. M. Rhea, B. Alvar, L. Burkett, S. Ball (2003). A Meta-Analysis to Determine the Dose Response for Strength Development. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise — Empirical meta-analysis on optimal training-volume/intensity for strength gains.
7. L. Kreher, J. Schwartz (2012). Overtraining Syndrome: A Practical Guide. Sports Health — Foundational overtraining-syndrome recognition and recovery-monitoring reference.
8. J. Daniels (2013). Daniels' Running Formula (3rd ed.). Human Kinetics — Standard applied running-training methodology reference (VDOT-based training zones).
9. P. Jack Daniels, N. Gerber (1974). Oxygen Consumption and Efficiency of White and Black College Runners. Applied research underlying running-economy training theory.
10. D. Docherty, D. Sporer (2000). A Proposed Model for Examining the Interference Phenomenon Between Concurrent Aerobic and Strength Training. Sports Medicine — Research on concurrent-training program design.
11. K. Baar (2014). Using Molecular Biology to Maximize Concurrent Training. Sports Medicine — Modern research informing combined strength/endurance program design.
12. G. Haff, N. Triplett (eds.) (2016). Essentials of Strength Training and Conditioning (4th ed., NSCA). Human Kinetics — Standard NSCA certification-level strength-training reference.
13. J. Fry (2004). The Role of Resistance Exercise Intensity on Muscle Fibre Adaptations. Sports Medicine — Research on training-intensity effects relevant to program design.
14. American College of Sports Medicine (2009). Progression Models in Resistance Training for Healthy Adults. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise — Standard evidence-based progressive-overload guideline position stand.
15. A. Jeukendrup, M. Gleeson (2018). Sport Nutrition (3rd ed.). Human Kinetics — Referenced for general recovery/fueling principles while deliberately avoiding prescriptive numeric targets in the skill's actual output.
16. P. Laursen, D. Jenkins (2002). The Scientific Basis for High-Intensity Interval Training: Optimising Training Programmes and Maximising Performance in Highly Trained Endurance Athletes. Sports Medicine — Foundational interval-training research.
17. W. Kraemer, N. Ratamess (2004). Fundamentals of Resistance Training: Progression and Exercise Prescription. Medicine & Science in Sports & Exercise — Standard resistance-training prescription framework.
18. T. Noakes (2003). Lore of Running (4th ed.). Human Kinetics — Comprehensive applied running-physiology and training reference.
19. S. Bird, K. Tarpenning, F. Marino (2005). Designing Resistance Training Programmes to Enhance Muscular Fitness: A Review of the Acute Programme Variables. Sports Medicine — Review of acute training-variable manipulation for program design.
20. National Academy of Sports Medicine (NASM) (2021). Essentials of Personal Fitness Training (7th ed.). NASM — Standard certified-trainer curriculum reference, cited to reinforce the recommendation to consult a certified professional.
